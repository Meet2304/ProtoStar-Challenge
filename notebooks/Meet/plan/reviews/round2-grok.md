## RISKS

- The delete gates were chosen with 2011–2018 in view. The only deletion they produce, Greenville-Spartanburg 2018-04-28, is after every backtest origin, so the skill table does not contain it. It changes one production training year by about +0.07 °F on Avg Temp.
- A city-block interval on a spatially coherent temperature gain will clear zero too often. Bonferroni does not repair that.
- `MIN_GAIN = 0.002` is the previous floor, and §5 still prints the old temperature gain 0.096 → 0.100. The new step estimator will change that gain. The bootstrap can still reject the variant. The floor was not set blind.
- `combo_model.METHOD` reads 30 years (10 for the blend’s short mean). Stuck deletions are in 1902–1931. The six precip days the neighbour test would delete are in 1898–1919. Dropped fragments end by 1903. Skill does not measure those edits. A later model that fits the full history would be using untested repairs.
- Training on QC or homogenised history predicts official labels that still contain the raw errors. That is the right target. It is a different series from the inputs whenever a repair falls inside the 30-year window.
- Filled CDD and filled precipitation include days the official label skips, so they run high. Only the backtest decides whether they become `<metric>_input`.
- Pairwise neighbour differences cannot see a network-wide instrument change. The plan states this. Those years are the years the blend and the 30-year average use.
- Huntsville has one valid temperature year in 1989–2018, so it is unscored, and the submission still needs a number. With the long block kept and infill not selected, the all-history fallback is the 1895–1957 climate plus 2018.
- `--skip-backtest` reuses the last `backtest_inputs.csv` choice. A constants change plus that flag ships an untested variant.
- Infill evidence (a) still answers “how well does the offset fit a baseline that saw the future of the hidden year.” Quoting it next to (b) will make the method look more accurate than the past-only number.
