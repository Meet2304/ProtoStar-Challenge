## MUST-FIX

1. **Resolve the official Avg Temp precision contract before implementation.** Use the published annual target, masked exactly as `prepare_data.py` does, as the authoritative label and require exact agreement with `annual_valid.csv` including the NaN mask. Keep a separate daily-rebuilt audit/QC value. Alternatively, explicitly reproduce and verify the original multi-stage rounding convention before asserting daily reconstruction equality. Do not merely loosen the official-label equality tolerance or round the final daily mean to two decimals: that alone does not reproduce the published target. Separate tolerances for a reconstruction audit from invariants on the actual saved labels; explain precision-only differences separately from repair/deletion differences in section 6 check 5.

   Read-only verification: among 24,583 city-years passing the official temperature validity rule, `(mean(tmax) + mean(tmin))/2` differs from the published `Avg Temp` by more than 0.000500001 in **22,219** rows; maximum absolute difference is **0.0098907104 degrees F**. Rounding that daily-derived mean to two decimals still disagrees in **5,983** rows. For example, Baton Rouge 1972 rebuilds to 69.3101092896 while the published target is 69.32. Published annual Avg Temp masked/rounded according to `prepare_data.py` matches `annual_valid.csv` on every row. This is a precision/provenance problem, not evidence for deleting observations.

2. **Replace the claimed confirmation with an honest selection/evaluation design, and fix early-origin calibration.** Define an outer chronological evaluation in which forecaster choice, input-variant choice, and fill-method calibration use only data available before each outer origin, with all choices frozen before scoring that outer test period. Use disjoint outer outcome periods for the performance claim. The current rotated origins can remain a sensitivity analysis. Because the full record and v0 results have already been inspected, disclose that the retrospective analysis is not a pristine untouched holdout; do not describe rotated windows as one. Either move initial fill calibration to 1982 or earlier, remove the origin-1982 claim, or select the fill method within each origin's training data.

   Main outcome years are 1987-2018; confirmation outcome years are 1983-2014. Their overlap is **28/32 confirmation years**. Origins 1990, 1998, and 2006 contribute no new outcome years. The four new years, 1983-1986, are inside D6's fill-selection period. The two sets therefore cannot supply the proposed independent confirmation.

   For the additional percentile features, distinguish archived published indices from forecast-safe inputs. At origins before 1987, recompute the thresholds from data available by that origin or exclude those columns from model inputs. This requirement concerns feature thresholds, not changes to the official labels.

3. **Allow the cleaning to lose to official inputs.** Make `official` an eligible default and require a positive, uncertainty-supported gain over it before selecting `qc` or a more elaborate input recipe. Retain all QC outputs for inspection even when they are not chosen as defaults. Compare every candidate on exactly the same official city-year mask, require finite predictions on every eligible row, and fail on silent row loss. `common.mae()` drops pairs with missing predictions, so merely invoking it does not enforce fair comparison. Report both the incremental gain over `qc` and the total gain over official inputs. 4. **Bootstrap the actual decision statistic with spatial dependence represented.** In each paired draw, resample predefined spatial groups of cities, retain each sampled city's entire sequence across origins, recompute each origin's raw-baseline denominator and candidate MAE on the common mask, and then average the per-origin skill differences with the same weights used by the selection rule. ## RISKS

- **Scope of this review:** I read the full v2 plan, project rules, dataset card, earlier review, scoring/validity code, and relevant v0 implementation. I ran read-only local Python on the four raw subsets and official labels, enforcing dates/years no later than 2018. I independently checked the target calculations, nine inversions, 18 CDD-valid years with more than ten incomplete temperature days, four empty station periods, and repeated-rain counts. I did not rerun v0's complete forecast experiment or execute the unwritten v2 pipeline. No downloads or post-2018 weather observations were used. Only this review file was written.
- The direct raw rebuild matches valid published precipitation and hottest-day values to floating-point precision and CDD exactly. Avg Temp is the specific failed equality claim; preserving authoritative labels resolves it without discarding the label/input separation.
- Recent station changes have short post-change samples. H3 is a legitimate improvement over v1: rebuilding at every origin does exercise the same endpoint rule. Reporting counts alone still does not establish that recent-step estimates are accurate.
- Current-site distances can be poor historical proxies, especially for coastal or mountainous cities. Spatial disagreement can represent real weather rather than measurement error. Pairwise methods also miss changes shared by their neighbours, as the plan correctly acknowledges.
- Filled precipitation/CDD contain days omitted by the official targets; station corrections and neighbour-infilled annual extremes can also alter target-relevant distributions. Keeping labels fixed, retaining provenance, and allowing official inputs to win are essential protections.
- An additive neighbour offset can produce physically inappropriate precipitation or CDD inputs, particularly for sparse/dry records. Specify nonnegative handling and audit boundary cases before exporting a selected infilled recipe.
- A seven-year forecast horizon is the deployment task, while the current tests use eight years. The extra year is not itself invalid, but report a prespecified seven-year sensitivity and performance by lead time without using that sensitivity to choose another winner.
- No retrospective backtest can prove improvement for all later feature engineering and models. The proposed selection is evidence about the specified forecaster and official scoring target; retain the alternative input columns so subsequent models can be assessed with the same selection discipline.
