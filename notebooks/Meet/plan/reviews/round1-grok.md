## RISKS

- Training on the QC-revised columns optimises a different label than the 2019–2025 scorer. The damage already in the current file is small (one CDD year in 1917, two Avg Temp years in 1989–2018, three early years flipped to NaN) and it is entirely from deletion, which a swap removes for the inversions.
- The Huntsville rule in v0 already leaves one year (2018) in `annual_clean.parquet`. Shipping that silently replaces ~50 years of climatology with a single year for a city the submission still has to forecast.
- An iid t that adopts 283 of 382 series will take regional trend differences into the "station step" and flatten the trend the blend forecaster is scored on. Neighbour differencing removes the common trend; it does not remove a coast-versus-inland trend over a 10-year window.
- A7, once coded loosely, can be selected by a 3-of-4 rule whose null hit rate is about 30% per variant. That can lower the 2019 forecast.
- Riverside 2009 and Natchitoches 2010 will enter the delivered homog series and did not enter the origin-2010 series the backtest scored. Skill for temperature homogenisation is not evidence about those two steps.
- Filled CDD and filled precip totals include days the official label skips, so they run high relative to the label. Only the backtest stands between that bias and the training input.
- Robust z is pooled across station changes and across years after the origin. A deletion decision for an early day can depend on the 2011–2018 climate.
- "15 nearest common valid years" includes years after the gap. That is safe only when every one of those years is ≤ the origin. The current `neighbour_infill` caps at `end_year`; the plan text does not.
- A `final_action = delete` in the review CSV changes inputs, and under the written target rule it would also change labels, with no new backtest.
- Treating the 0.01-inch runs as accumulated storm totals and "correcting" them would bias `ToT Prcp` for a problem the annual total does not have.
- C2 picks the Abilene month by maximising \|t\| on the same temperature series the step is then fit to. The overlap is in 1943–44, before every origin, so the test windows do not leak. The date itself is overfit. Low stakes, one city.
- Documented moves after about 2006 and any ASOS shift shared with the neighbours stay in the last 30 years. Those are the years the blend and the 30-year average actually use. The plan's pairwise adjustment does not touch the shared ASOS shift.
